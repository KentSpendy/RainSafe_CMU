<!-- ? Objective -->

the general objective of this project is to develop a web-based application that provides real-time rainfall and wind monitoring to support the safety and preparedness of the CMU (Central Mindanao University) Philippines community. Specifically, it aimed to:

Design a user interface that allows students, faculty, and residents to view real-time weather data on a map focused on CMU (Central Mindanao University) Philippines.
Integrate the OpenWeatherMap and Open-Mateo APIs for accessing localized weather conditions such as rainfall probability, temperature, and wind speed.
Implement automated in-app alerts when rainfall probability exceeds 70% or wind conditions reach unsafe levels.
Design a reporting feature that allows users to pin their location, describe the incident or concern, and submit their report quickly.

<!--? Scope and Limitations -->

The scope of this study is limited to the design and development of a rain monitoring and alert system specifically for Central Mindanao University (CMU). The system features include login authentication, user and admin dashboards, map-based visualization, weather monitoring, and automated in-app notifications and reporting feature.
The system uses OpenStreetMap as the base map, OpenWeatherMap for rainfall and wind overlays, and Open-Meteo for retrieving live, historical, and forecasted weather data. Leaflet.js enables the map’s interactivity, allowing users to navigate and control layers. However, the study is limited by the API’s update frequency and accuracy. The alert system is restricted to in-app notifications only, as SMS integration is not included in this study. Furthermore, since the application is web-based, it requires internet connectivity, and unstable connections may affect the delivery of updates.
While RainSafe CMU aims to reduce the impact of rainfall disruptions in academic and daily activities, external factors such as user adoption, personal preparedness, and other environmental conditions may still affect outcomes.
